    How the Process Works

    The steps shared by both paths - the details unique to each path are listed above.

    1. 1

      Getting acquainted & checking fit

      We'll get to know each other, understand your story, and choose the right path.

    2. 2

      A shared session or conversation

    Transparency Before We Begin

    Good to Know

    • Every piece is original and made especially for you.
    • Prices do not include framing or shipping.
    • Framing services can be added by prior arrangement.
    • The delivery date is set before work begins.
    • I work on a limited number of commissions at a time - if there's a backlog, I'll let you know in advance how it affects the delivery date.
    • The meditation stage can also be held remotely. In an in-person session, you can place the first line on the piece yourself.
    • You don't need to know in advance what the piece will look like - it takes shape through the process itself.
    • The commission enters the process after confirmation and scheduling.
    Frequently Asked Questions

    Questions About the Private Commission

    Is the private commission open right now?

    Yes, you can submit a proposal at any time.

    How do I submit a proposal?

    Send a short message of interest with a bit of information about you and the piece you're imagining. I get back personally to those selected to continue.

    What kinds of commissions are open?

    Shamanic spirit portraits, personal power pieces, visionary stories, book illustrations, and other unique ideas.

    Is it first-come, first-served?

    No. A limited number of commissions are accepted at any given time, based on fit for the process.

    How long does it take?

    Usually 3 to 12 months, depending on the scope of the piece and the current commission load.

    Why does the duration vary?

    Every piece develops at its own pace, and the number of active commissions at the same time also affects the timeline.

    Is a deposit required?

    Payment and deposit details are arranged personally with whoever is selected to continue in the process.

    Can I submit a unique or unconventional idea?

    Absolutely - some of the most interesting pieces begin with an unconventional idea.

    What happens if my proposal is selected?

    I'll get back to you to schedule a shared meditation, and from there the piece begins to develop.
