    How it works

    Progress without
    losing your freedom.

    This isn't a classroom where everyone paints the same thing. Each participant develops their own personal work and receives guidance from me tailored exactly to the stage they're at.

    You can experiment with graphite, colored pencils, pastels, and acrylic, continue a project you've already started, or open up a new direction. I help with choosing materials, planning the work, technique, and decisions that come up along the way.

    No prior experience is required. The workshop suits both those who simply want to bring creativity into their routine and those who already create and want a consistent framework for growth.
