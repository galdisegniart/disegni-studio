    The Process

    From getting to know the material
    to a language of your own.

    The track combines clear structure with personal freedom: there's something to learn in every session, but no single piece everyone has to copy.

    We'll experiment with graphite, colored pencils, pastels, and acrylic. We'll get to know the properties of each material, practice techniques, and learn how to choose the right approach for the idea you want to express.

    Throughout the process I guide each participant personally, from the initial idea, through composition and color, to the small decisions that turn a piece of work into a creation with real presence.
