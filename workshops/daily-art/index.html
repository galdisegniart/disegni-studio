    What awaits you

    Connect through
    color and material.

    I invite you into a free, held space where you can simply be and explore painting as a tool for personal expression.

    We'll work with graphite, colored pencils, pastels, and acrylic. You can choose a direction, try a new material, or simply start with a single mark and see where your hand leads you.

    There's no right or wrong way to paint. There's exploration, enjoyment, and personal guidance that lets everyone progress at their own pace.

    A glimpse inside

    This is what
    it feels like.

    Watch moments from the workshop - the gathering, the materials, and the freedom to discover your own way within the creative process.

    Everything you need to get started
    Personal guidance

    You're never alone with the page

    I move between participants, listening and offering precise direction when needed.

    All materials included

    Just show up

    Graphite, pencils, pastels, and acrylic are ready on site, along with quality paper to work on.

    An intimate space

    A small, pleasant group

    3-6 participants, music, drinks, and light refreshments - no crowding, no pressure.

    A piece to take home

    Something personal that stays with you

    Every participant leaves with a personal painting, framed in a way that honors what was created.
